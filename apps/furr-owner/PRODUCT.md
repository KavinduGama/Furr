# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

adaptive

## Users

Pet owners who need a dependable, calm place to keep a companion's health history, complete everyday care, and share the record with a veterinary professional.

## Product Purpose

FURR gives an owner one home for a pet's profile, care routines, health records, documents, reminders, and sharing. Success is that the next care task is obvious and the health record is ready when it matters.

## Positioning

FURR connects the day-to-day care view with an exportable, shareable health record for the same pet.

## Operating Context

Owners use the product in short mobile sessions: at home while giving care, while adding a record after an appointment, or when retrieving a document for a vet. The primary workflow starts with a pet, then moves between Today, Pets, Care, and Profile.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Expo Router app that ships to iOS, Android, and web.
- Portrait-first, compact navigation with four top-level sections.
- Existing routes, form field names, authentication, Firebase persistence, and preview mode must remain functional.
- Health data can be owner-entered, document-backed, or vet-verified.

## Brand Commitments

- Product name: FURR.
- A warm, calm, trustworthy companion-health experience rather than a clinical dashboard.
- Existing teal brand family and real pet photography are available assets.

## Evidence on Hand

- Real local images: `assets/furr/max-hero-editorial.png`, `assets/furr/luna-portrait-editorial.png`, and `assets/furr/omega-softgel-editorial.png`.
- Current functional mobile routes and realistic preview-pet data.
- No validated marketing claims, testimonials, or clinical outcomes are available. Do not invent them.

## Product Principles

- Make the next useful care action easy to spot.
- Treat health information as trusted, legible recordkeeping.
- Keep routine moments reassuring, not alarmist.
- Preserve platform-native navigation and control behavior.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- Support readable text, clear labels, meaningful errors, and touch targets appropriate to iOS and Android.
- Avoid color-only status signals and honor platform text scaling where supported by React Native.
